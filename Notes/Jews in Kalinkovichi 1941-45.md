### Scientists are investigating the past of the area

Until the early 1990s in the USSR and the BSSR the topic of the Holocaust at the official level was not actually studied. This is evidenced by the analysis of all volumes of “Belarusian Savetkai Entsylapedi”, which was published in the 1970-ies, and other official literature. Holocaust victims were officially called “peaceful Soviet citizens” without nationality.

As an example, we can call the article “Kalinkavitsky Raiyen” in the fifth volume “Belaruskay Savetskaya Entsylapedyi”, (Mn., 1972), where even the word “yaurei” is missing.

Confusion is also allowed in the book “Pamats: Gystoryka-dcumentary chronicle of Kalinkavitskaga Rayon” (Mn., 1999).

Thus, on page 232 it is stated that Elya Khaimovich Atlas was shot on June 25, 1941 - on the fourth day of the war - long before the city of Kalinkovichi was occupied by the Nazis, ten-year-old Leonid Belenky died on May 28, 1944, that is, a few months after the liberation of the city, and 70-year-old Tavel Yankelevich Peysakhovich was shot later.

During the Great Patriotic War, more than 3 million residents of Belarus were killed - every third resident of the BSSR. In 1941-1944, about 715 thousand Belarusian Jews and about 90 thousand foreign Jews from 7 countries of Europe became victims of the Holocaust.

Among them were hundreds of Jews in the city of Kalinkovichi and Kalinkovichi district. Although there are several publications on this topic, there are still “white spots” and unknown pages. The main sources remain the funds of the National Archives of the Republic of Belarus, the Zonal State Archives in Mozyr, the State Archive of the Russian Federation, the Israeli National Institute of Remembrance of the Victims of the Shoah (Catastrophe) and heroism Yad Vashem, eyewitness accounts, materials of periodicals, not to mention encyclopedias and reference books.

It is hard to believe, but according to the All-Russian census of 1897, 1341 Jews lived in Kalinkovichi, which was 100% of the population. In 1923 the number of Jews was 2612, in 1926 - 3102 (50.4%), in 1939 - 3386 (34.6% of the population).

On August 21 (according to other sources, August 22), 1941, parts of the Wehrmacht were occupied by Kalinkovichi. Vinnytsia, the number of Jews among the refugees mobilized and evacuees, was 79.3% in the city of Kalinkovichi. The solitary killings of Jews began in early September 1941. On September 21, 1941, _Tsedik (Tsadik) Karasik_, Boris Kofman, Dvosya Livszyts, Maria Minevich-Aisenstat, Khana Minevich, Ginda Uretskaya, Gita & Marat Freulakhman, 75-year-old Vyacheslav, and David Shapiro.

On the eve of the mass shooting of the Jewish population of the Kalinkovichi district, it was occupied with unbearable labor. Miraculously, the survivor Vera Hechtman recalled that able-bodied Jews from Domanovich and Ozarich were sent to concentration camp No. 1 near the village of Oktyabrsky, where they were engaged in forestry.

In publications about the Holocaust in the city, it was noted that exactly a month later - on September 22, 1941, the Jews of Kalinkovich in the number of several hundred people were taken to the Dudich railway crossing and shot. About this shooting, the author of these lines heard different rumors and versions are sometimes contradictory.

Publishing the testimony of witnesses about the Nazi genocide against the residents of the Kalinkovichi district, official publishers forgot to indicate that Jews were primarily victims of this genocide.

A quarter of a century ago - in 1990 - I managed to find the Act of the Polesie Regional Commission on the crimes of the occupiers in the city of Kalinkovichi of December 15, 1944. In front of me there was a terrible picture of the Holocaust in the city of Kalinkovichi. Let me give you the fragments from this document:

On September 20, 1941, the German command posted an announcement in the city, which indicated that the entire Jewish population should move to the Dacha street of the city, which is separated from the residence of the latter. When the Jewish population moved to the street, a second order was given that the entire population from old to the young should arrive at the Kalinkovichi station at a meeting at which the issues of improving their financial situation would be discussed. The entire population was obliged by this order to dress in good clothes. The audience was cordoned off by German soldiers armed with machine guns and drove into the camp for barbed wire, and at dawn on September 22, 1941, the entire Jewish population in trucks was transported to the Dudich railway crossing, located 1.5 km northeast of the city and brutally shot by German machine gunners with the participation of traitors to the motherland, police officers. The corpses were dumped into a ravine of a railway dead end and sprinkled with earth.

On December 6, 1944, Soviet experts conducted an exhumation of graves, which showed that the kalinkovichi Jews were not only shot, but also severely beaten and buried alive. Some were killed by heavy objects on the head. Members of the Kalinkovichi district commission suggested that the ravine could be up to 700 corpses of Jews killed by the Nazis and their accomplices.

In the Act of the Polesie Regional Commission on the crimes of the occupiers in the city of Kalinkovichi, it is stated that the excavations of the grave of the victims of the fascist terror for the Dudich crossing are fully confirmed by the testimony of witnesses about the artistic nature of this mass shooting of the Jewish population. After 1945, some of the Jews returned to Kalinkovichi. In 1947 there were 1,460 Jews in the city, and in 1995 - about 300.

Hundreds of Jews in the Kalinkovichi district became victims of the Holocaust. So, on August 5, 1941, Nazis and policemen brought from the ghetto of the town of Ozarichi 262 of his prisoner, in most old people, women and children. They were all brutally murdered. Their graves are 500 metres from the Jewish cemetery.

On October 26, 1943, prison prisoners by order of the occupation authorities dug up corpses from three graves and burned them. The fact is that the Nazis had topographic maps on which the graves were indicated. All prisoners were destroyed after the end of this work.

In September 1941, 4 Germans came from Kalinkovich to the village of Sitny Dushchsky village council. Together with the countryside, they gathered 18 Jews, mostly old men, women and children. Under the convoy, they were taken out of the village and they were shot on the edge of the forest. The property of the Jews was looted by locals. A memorial plaque is installed on the mass grave of these Jews.

On the eve of the Jewish pogroms in the town of Yurovichi, violence against women was carried out. Thus, the soldiers of the arriving punitive detachment in mid-September 1941 raped two Jews.

In the first days of November 1941, by order of the chief of police, the town of Yurovichi Kozhemyako and his deputy Emelianov, the population of Jewish nationality was placed on Podgornaya Street in several families in each house. When Jews resettle from their own homes, the police, by order of the German authorities, together with the Germans, robbed property, including livestock belonging to local Jews

After resettlement, the Jews were not allowed to leave this street, and those who left were shot. The Jews on their left sleeve had to wear bandages made of yellow material.

On November 18, 1941, 20 policemen arrived from Kalinkovich in Yurovichi in cars, who, together with the Jurist police on November 19, 1941, early in the morning drove about 200 people of different ages to the bazaar area. Then the team under the threat of weapons was driven to the outskirts of the town of Yurovichi, where on the shore of the river. Everyone was shot. To shoot innocent people, the Germans used a machine gun and other weapons. The corpses of the dead were thrown into the tear of the animals and were not buried for 7 days.

On November 27, 1941, and then in early December 1941, the Germans committed the second and third pogroms of the Jewish population in this town. People were also kicked out to the bazaar square, and then taken to the moats on the outskirts of Yurović and shot. According to the members of the commission to investigate the atrocities of the German occupiers in the Yurovichi village council and the representative of the Kalinkovichi district commission, recorded in the act of December 9, 1944, "German monsters, their accomplices and executors killed more than 400 people, including 207 children, 128, of which more than 100 people are sick."
