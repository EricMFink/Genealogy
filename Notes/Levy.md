## Flora Levy

b. ca. 1813

## Sarah Levy

b. ca. 1855

d. 16 April 1899 (age ~45) in Titusville

Husband Solomon abandoned her in "Russia" and went to US. She tracked him down in Edensberg, PA, where he was married to another woman. He then fled the country.

No other known relatives in the US.
