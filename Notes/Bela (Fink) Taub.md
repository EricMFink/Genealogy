## Jana Dohnalová, Tajemná židovská komunita od baroka po šoa

### Rodina Fink 

> The Fink family came from Budislav, the first mentioned is Bela Fink -- born 1812, her husband Lejb Taub owned house no. 110 in Dobra Voda[^1], where he ran a baking business. She was his second wife after the death of Miriam Taub in 1800. 
> 
> Apparently the brother of Bela, Mochl Fink, died in 1863. 

[^1]: Dobra Voda: about midway between Černovice & Nová Cerekev

### Rodina Taub 

Another branch of the Taub family lived in Dobra Voda, where Lejb Taub owned house no. 110, where he ran a baking business. His first wife, Miriam, dies in 1800, he was married again to Bela, born Fink in Budislav in 1812. Lejb died in 1835.
